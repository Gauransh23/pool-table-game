Modifications: public HQ MP3 previews decoded to mono PCM 44.1 kHz/16 bit, separate takes trimmed, DC removed, short onset/end fades, early-body RMS gain matching with a -4 dBFS sample-peak ceiling. No pitch shifting or added synthesis/reverb. Exact offsets/gains/hashes: `recorded_contacts.json`; exact reproducible recipe: `tools/generate_audio.py`.

KVV Audio describes Zoom H5/XYH-5 capture, original 96 kHz/24-bit stereo. Our source is the publicly supplied **lossy HQ preview**, not the original high-resolution WAV. Rail is a Nokia 6125i phone recording and only **one take**; it is a provisional quality/variation limit. The other three classes have five distinct recorded onsets each. Human naturalness/material recognition remains unverified.

`sources/*.json` preserves exact original page, preview URL, licence, retrieval/archive metadata and raw hashes, including research candidates not used in gameplay. Original preview bytes and original HTML snapshots are kept outside the distributable game; the edited gameplay assets and this attribution accompany the source. `baseline/` preserves the previous locally authored synthetic files and generator for comparison/rollback, not active playback.
