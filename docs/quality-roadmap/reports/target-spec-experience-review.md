Disposition: **CONDITIONAL** for the specification; **all gameplay targets remain UNVERIFIED**. This is a review of proposed acceptance criteria, not an execution result or achieved-quality claim.

Scope: aim/input, power selection, stroke perception, visuals, audio, performance, and human comparison. ## What is already strong

- **PASS, specification discipline:** every requirement is UNVERIFIED. EVD-004 forbids extracting physical coefficients or latency from edited GIFs. AUD-001 requires actual listening and HUM-004 requires identified playable references. This correctly preserves the reference access/audio limitations.
- **PASS, units and causality:** PWR-001 separates authored power, cue speed, impulse and ball state. Current `scripts/sim/stroke_power.gd` defines `MAX_CUE_SPEED = 8.0`; `CueStrikeModel.apply` derives ball speed separately. PWR-004's 8 m/s cue-speed wording is consistent with current source. Neither a percentage nor displayed cue animation establishes newtons or contact force.
- **PASS, demanding coverage:** correct GUI routing, a precise input path, fine distance selection, physical tip/strike correspondence, temporal art stability, actual shipped performance, dense audio behavior and independent perception axes are explicit targets. These are materially stronger than still-image approval or headless throughput.

## Required final acceptance packet

The executing agent should receive an executable distinction between these evidence layers:

1. **Machine reachability:** exact input states, guide reprojection, cue/ball mappings and deterministic lifecycle. Test exported interaction paths as well as isolated functions.
2. **Presented interaction:** timestamped physical input and correct displayed state, native mouse and trackpad separately, across the frozen logical-window/framebuffer matrix. Synthetic dispatch timestamps cannot substitute for physical-input latency.
3. **Perceived result:** gameplay-scale blinded identity/material/strength tests, motion review, actual listening, and participant-level task/rating data. Instrumentation does not replace experience.
4. **Reference comparison:** legitimate identified Miniclip and second-reference tasks, recorded unknowns, matched assistance/table/cue/device conditions or an explicit mismatch analysis. Existing silent edited tutorials remain design references only.
5. **Release performance:** installed-artifact hash, repeatable task script, stage/present traces, thermal state, failures/exclusions and raw evidence. Fixed-FPS export cannot certify real-time cadence.

Each result must name its requirement IDs, exact scope, expected and actual values, uncertainty, status and raw artifacts. An inaccessible comparator, unavailable calibrated timing rig or absent listening panel blocks only the applicable evidence claim; it never licenses an invented pass. For product-wide completion every required open claim still needs resolution.

Remaining concrete conditions:

1. **Unify PER-003 with F-PERFORMANCE in the authoritative JSON.** At 60 Hz, T=16.666… ms: the catalog says p95<=17.17 and intervals>33.33 ms, while protocols say p95<=T+0.5 and intervals>2T. An exact two-refresh interval is counted by one rule and not the other. Author the T formulas once, state measurement/timestamp tolerance and exact higher-refresh budgets before those modes are supported, then regenerate the category page. This resolves a classification discrepancy without changing the intended bar.
2. **Make HUM-005 and scored-trial rules prospectively operational.** Before recruitment/scored collection, freeze familiarization, at least two scored learning blocks, target/shot randomization, ready-stimulus/commit timestamps, retries/missing responses, expertise definition and fatigue breaks. State participant-level success and landing-error/variance estimands, non-degradation margins and interval decision rules. Require no unexplained sign mismatch independently of favorable averages. A recorded plan that says only 'stable or improves' is insufficient.
3. **Freeze recognition and intensity study parameters before collection.** Specify sample/trial counts, timed response, required strata and the uncertainty-aware pass rule for VIS-002/AIM-006/FEL-002/AUD-002/003. Determine calibrated playback level and a reproducible ambient-noise condition for the softest-shot audibility task. Neither an invented perceptual threshold nor a post hoc expanded deck should certify a pass.

These conditions concern acceptance procedure precision; they do not call for lowering numerical, visual, audio or perception targets. Parent/executor can resolve them in the catalog/protocol before acceptance runs. Absence of calibrated timing equipment, participants or playable references is an execution-evidence blocker, not proof that the specification itself fails.
