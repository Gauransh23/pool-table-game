## Spec verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED (one bounded visible defect)

The candidate substantially satisfies the requested bounded feature: active strike/stepper/config replay on cloned balls, signed spin and cloth evolution, distinct cue/object paths, honest planar-speed units and sampling scope, cluster/bank-first qualification, per-ball subsequent-interaction truncation and bounded horizons. Hollow ring and edged ivory/cyan paths match the requested representation. Cache invalidation includes ball canonical states and authored config/table values; camera and viewport changes reproject world paths without rerunning physics. Existing input/save/cancel behavior has focused regression evidence.

**[P2] Keep the contact ring at contact, rather than the tick-end cue center.** `scripts/sim/outgoing_guide.gd:92-98` assigns `result.contact = _cue.position` after the whole fixed step, and `scripts/presentation/table_presentation.gd:159` draws that as the contact ring. A fast cut can move the cue substantially after the contact event within the same tick. This is distinct from, and not repaired by, accurately labeling the speed readout as tick-end speeds. Diagnostic source `/tmp/pool-guide-review-event-probe.gd` subclasses the active PhysicsStepper solely to read the cue center at `_record_event(ball_contact)` and compares it with the tick endpoint. With default config, cue at (-0.35,0.12), target at (0,0.17), zero spin and cue speed 8 m/s: contact-event cue center (-0.022624,0.119120), ring center (-0.014804,0.114725), displacement **8.97 mm**. The marker visibly shifts downstream of contact. The separate straight-line geometric comparison also differs by 13.91 mm, but that comparison includes existing solver contact approximation and is not the basis of this defect. Run: `bash tools/godot.sh --headless --path . --script /tmp/pool-guide-review-event-probe.gd`, exit 0. Preserve first-contact cue center as additive event metadata, or capture it by a replay-only adapter, and use it for the ring; keep speeds/path sample meaning explicit. Do not reconstruct cue center by blindly subtracting one radius from the existing event midpoint because actual solver separation/normal can differ from ideal contact. Add a hard-cut fixture requiring ring position equal the recorded contact center and distinct from endpoint when the cue moves afterward. No solver replacement or coefficient change is needed.
